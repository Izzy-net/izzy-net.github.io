Ghoul Buster Training Simulator

Ghoul Buster Training Simulator is a comedic 3D visual puzzle game with fps mechanics. I worked solo going from concept to completed and polished game in 2 months, evolving the mechanics and design to better fulfil the desired player experience: comedic pattern recognition game.

Learning shaders has been a very interesting endeavour, and has opened up a lot of possibilites for enhancing artistic direction. The three shaders I am most proud of are shown above. The paper shader is applied to a plane mesh that creases according to a paper texture normal map. The paper texture and a 'sketch' texture are conditionally mixed to create the final colour. The night vision shader I created for Ghoul Buster Training Simulator combines a flat colour tint, perception weighted glow and blur, with a mask to create the final screen effect which can be animated via the shader parameters. The watercolour shader is in development for my current cozy game project and uses displacement and moving noise to undulate the colour texture, and a second shader draws the screen texture onto a 2D paper texture object, darkening the screen colours where there are creases in the paper.
